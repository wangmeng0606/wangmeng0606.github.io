"Influence of zwitterionic amphiphilic copolymers on heterogeneous gypsum formation: a promising approach for scaling resistance," the final chapter of my Ph.D. dissertation. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.watres.2024.122439)

Accumulated precipitate mass alone cannot separate nucleation from crystal growth, so I combined time-resolved synchrotron X-ray scattering with longer-term QCM-D measurements to resolve each. Working with Prof. Ayse Asatekin's group at Tufts University and beamline scientists at Argonne National Laboratory, we found that ZAC coatings containing MPC increased nucleation density relative to SBMA-containing coatings while inhibiting crystal growth over longer times, which slowed the overall kinetics. Separating these processes links a mechanistic finding to a coating design strategy for scaling control.
