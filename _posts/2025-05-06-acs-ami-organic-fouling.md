"Organic fouling on zwitterionic amphiphilic copolymers: implications in biofouling." This study extends the ZAC coating platform from mineral scaling to biofouling, examining how organic conditioning changes coating surface properties and subsequent bacterial attachment and biofilm formation. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsami.5c07057)
