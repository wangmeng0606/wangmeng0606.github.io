Research

My research investigates how molecular interactions in aqueous and interfacial systems govern speciation, transport, and separation performance. I combine spectroscopy, thermodynamic modeling, and multiscale experiments to connect molecular-scale behavior with membrane separations, resource recovery, and scaling/fouling control at the water–energy nexus.
