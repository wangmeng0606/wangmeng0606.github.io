# Website Development Guidelines

This is a personal academic website for Meng Wang, Ph.D., built with Jekyll and GitHub Pages.

## Overall goal

Refine the existing website into a clean, modern, professional academic website suitable for faculty applications. Keep the existing Jekyll/GitHub Pages architecture.

Use the visual style of strong contemporary academic/research-group websites, including the Pan Lab website, as inspiration only. Do not copy its design or content.

## Priorities

### 1. Typography and visual design — HIGH PRIORITY
- Replace the current font with a clean modern sans-serif, preferably Inter or a similar font.
- Improve heading hierarchy, spacing, whitespace, and readability.
- Use a restrained academic color palette.
- Make the site feel modern, clean, and professional rather than like a generic Jekyll template.
- Ensure good desktop and mobile responsiveness.

### 2. Homepage — HIGH PRIORITY
Make the homepage immediately communicate:
- who Meng Wang is
- current position
- research focus
- broader research vision

Keep the core research themes:
- Ion Transport
- Interfacial Chemistry
- Membrane & Separation Systems

Do not unnecessarily rewrite the scientific content.

### 3. Research identity — HIGH PRIORITY
Present the research as one coherent program connecting:

Molecular interactions → speciation/transport → interfaces → membrane separations → water and energy sustainability.

Emphasize the connection between fundamental molecular science and practical separation/resource-recovery applications.

### 4. News — HIGH PRIORITY
Replace the emphasis on outdated 2023 updates with current academic news.

Prioritize:
- publications
- awards
- talks
- conferences
- research projects
- beamtime
- software
- funding/proposals

Do not invent information.

### 5. Publications — MEDIUM PRIORITY
Keep publications consolidated under **Peer-Reviewed Journal Articles** rather than creating many categories.

Improve readability, consistency, DOI links, and selected-publication presentation.

Do not invent or modify bibliographic information.

## Important rules

- Inspect the existing repository before making changes.
- Preserve existing content and functionality.
- Do not migrate away from Jekyll.
- Do not make large architectural changes unless necessary.
- Do not invent scientific information, publications, DOI numbers, awards, or news.
- Prefer simple, clean academic design over decorative effects.
- Before making major changes, explain the proposed changes briefly and then implement them.
