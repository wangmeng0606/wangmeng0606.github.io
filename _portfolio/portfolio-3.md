Ion pairing and hydration structure govern how ions move through—and interact with—aqueous and polymer systems, from simple electrolyte solutions to hydrated membrane materials. My current work, with Professor Lynn Katz at The University of Texas at Austin, uses spectroscopy to resolve ion pairing and hydration structure in sulfate electrolytes spanning monovalent to trivalent cations.

This work aims to connect molecular-scale ion pairing and hydration structure to macroscopic sorption and transport behavior, providing a more accurate basis for predicting ion behavior in engineered water systems.
