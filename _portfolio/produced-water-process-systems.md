Alongside the fundamental work above, I contribute to applied, process-scale research on produced-water treatment. The pilot system pairs a hollow-fiber membrane for selective oil recovery with a ceramic membrane for suspended-solids removal. My role is evaluating membrane performance through solution-chemistry analysis and surface characterization, so that performance results are tied to a mechanistic explanation of how the process works. I am now starting a new project on electrocoagulation of highly saline waters.
